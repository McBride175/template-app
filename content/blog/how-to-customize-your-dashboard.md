## Start With Outcome Metrics
Lead with cash collected today, cash collected per collector hour, and average touches per resolved invoice.

## Build a Return-Based Work Queue
Sort accounts by expected recoverable cash divided by expected effort. This keeps the team focused on high-yield actions.

## Show the Next Best Action
For each account, display one clear recommendation such as send reminder, call, escalate dispute, or pause follow-up.

## Iterate With Real Usage
Review where reps spend time, which actions resolve fastest, and where disputes stall collection cycles.
