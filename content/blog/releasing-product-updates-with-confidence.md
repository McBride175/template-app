## Define Performance Guardrails Early
Set expected targets for recovery rate, average days to payment, and collector time per resolved invoice before launch.

## Test Critical Collection Journeys
Validate first reminder, follow-up cadence, dispute handling, promised-to-pay tracking, and payment confirmation flows.

## Roll Out by Segment
Start with a low-risk customer segment, compare against a control group, then expand once metrics improve.

## Monitor the First Full Collection Cycle
Track payment outcomes, overdue movement, and rep feedback through at least one full reminder cycle before broad rollout.
