## Write for Collection Moments
Organize docs around concrete moments like first reminder, no-response follow-up, dispute escalation, and payment promise tracking.

## Keep Scripts Short and Reusable
Provide concise message templates that reps can adapt quickly without rewriting each outreach from scratch.

## Include Exception Paths
Document what to do when a payer disputes, partially pays, requests terms, or goes silent after promising payment.

## Update from Outcomes
After each cycle, refine playbooks using win/loss reasons so documented steps reflect what actually improves recovery.
